# Provisional Sol model policy

GPT-5.6-Sol with reasoning `medium` is the provisional default for drafting and
both translator-owned revision passes. GPT-5.6-Sol `high` is not a parallel
default and must not silently replace medium. Use high only to adjudicate the
specific title or critical/major findings left after the second V4 Pro review.

Keep high as the comparison baseline on two additional texts of different
genres. Use the same frozen Chinese, source analysis, terminology decisions,
two Pro reviews, anonymous rubric, and human adjudication of decisive
term/title disagreements. Promote medium from provisional to permanent only if
it wins at least one additional text and its three-text average critical/major
blocker count is no worse than high. Until then, report the policy as
"medium provisional default; high targeted fallback," not as a universal model
ranking.
